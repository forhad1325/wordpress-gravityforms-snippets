# Gravity Forms Email Checker

Validates submitted emails against a public Google Sheet CSV allowlist before allowing form submission.

## Usage

1. Paste code into your theme's `functions.php` or a custom plugin.
2. Replace `YOUR_SHEET_ID` with your Google Sheet ID.
3. Update the form IDs (`gform_validation_36`, `gform_validation_37`) and email field ID (`2`) to match your setup.

## Requirements

- Gravity Forms
